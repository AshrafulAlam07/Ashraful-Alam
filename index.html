About Me

Interested in AI that is reliable, safe, and interpretable

AI Researcher

I am a recent Computer Science graduate from BRAC University with research interests in trustworthy artificial intelligence, federated learning, medical AI, vision-language models, and LLM security. My undergraduate thesis investigated a privacy-preserving federated learning framework for remote patient monitoring.

I am also involved in research on trustworthy vision-language models for medical image understanding and geometric defenses against prompt poisoning in federated LoRA systems. I am particularly interested in understanding how AI systems behave under uncertainty, adversarial pressure, privacy constraints, and limited computational resources.

My long-term goal is to pursue doctoral research focused on developing reliable and interpretable AI systems for high-stakes applications.
